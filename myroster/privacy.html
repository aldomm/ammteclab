Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

myRoster is developed by Aldo Mendes Martins, operating as AMM Tec Lab. Contact aldoapps@gmail.com for support and privacy requests. This policy covers myRoster and the pages under ammteclab.com/myroster/.

Credentials and sign-in

Your Crew ID and password are stored in a non-synchronizing, device-only iOS Keychain item. During sign-in they are sent over HTTPS to your roster website to authenticate your account. The app developer does not operate a server that receives these credentials. Device authentication is required before automatic login. The app receives an authentication result, not your Face ID or Touch ID biometric data.

roster website receives your authentication details and network requests and may process account identifiers, IP addresses, session information and activity under its own policies. myRoster uses a temporary WebKit session. After a successful sync saves all requested periods, it attempts server logout and clears that temporary session. Interrupted syncs may retain the session to allow Resume. A local session clear is not a guarantee of server-side logout.

Roster and crew information

The app retrieves the schedule and available duty details you are authorized to access. These can include dates, flight and duty times, crew names and identifiers, roles, aircraft, passenger counts, gates, locations and other information supplied by roster website. Saved rosters remain on your device, use iOS file protection and are excluded from device backups. They are not uploaded to an app-developer server. A new download replaces an existing saved roster for the same period. Older periods stay until you delete them.

Weather, ATIS and other services

Weather requests send airport ICAO codes to NOAA’s aviationweather.gov. Hong Kong ATIS is retrieved from atis.cad.gov.hk. These providers receive normal connection information, such as your IP address and request headers. myRoster does not send them your roster website credentials, crew names or saved roster files. Weather responses are cached temporarily in memory. Opening an external source link is subject to that site’s policies.

Settings, tracking and website hosting

Preferences, the last visible roster date and acknowledgement of usage notices are stored locally. The app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

This app website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical request logs for delivery and security under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These myRoster pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, forms or intentionally set cookies. Other pages on this domain and linked services may have different practices.

TestFlight and support

Apple processes TestFlight installation, usage, crash and feedback information under its TestFlight privacy information. The developer may receive beta feedback, screenshots and diagnostic reports supplied through Apple. If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and the content you send; email providers process that correspondence.

Do not include passwords, confidential documents or unnecessary personal information about yourself or other crew members in support messages or screenshots. Support correspondence and diagnostics are retained only as reasonably needed to answer requests, resolve issues, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations.

Purposes and sharing

Information is used to authenticate your authorized account, retrieve and display your roster, retain your chosen settings, deliver requested weather information and provide support. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Processing by the providers described above is necessary to deliver their services. If lawfully required, support information held by the developer may be disclosed to competent authorities.

Deletion and your choices

  • Delete individual months from Saved Rosters, or choose “Remove all saved rosters” in Settings.
  • Choose “Forget credentials and session” to remove the saved Keychain credentials and clear the local web session. Deleting the app alone should not be relied on to remove Keychain items.
  • Local deletion does not delete your airline account or records held by roster website, Apple or other independent providers. Contact the respective provider for those records.
  • For access, correction or deletion of support information held by the developer, contact aldoapps@gmail.com. We may request proportionate verification before handling a request.

Legal rights and international processing

Where applicable law grants rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to or receive a copy of personal data, or to withdraw consent or complain to a supervisory authority, you may exercise those rights subject to applicable exceptions. Requests can be sent to the support address above. Processing needed for requested services, legitimate security and support purposes, consent where required, and legal obligations may apply depending on the circumstances and jurisdiction.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their policies describe their arrangements. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; protect your device, keep it updated and use only accounts you are authorized to access.

Children and policy changes

myRoster is intended for adults with authorized access to an airline crew account; it is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact us. We may update this policy when functionality or practices change. The effective date will be updated, and materially new processing will be explained in the app where appropriate.
