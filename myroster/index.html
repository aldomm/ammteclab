Months together

Save available months locally. Select saved periods, combine them, or request the current month and previous eleven.

Details on demand

Open flights and duties for the information available from eCrew. Swipe between flights in the same duty.

Your device

Store credentials in the iOS Keychain. Sync in the foreground, then sign out after saving. Delete local data in Settings.

Weather and airport references are supplementary and may be stale, incomplete or incorrect. Do not use them for operational or safety-critical decisions.
