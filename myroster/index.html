The roster at a glance

A compact timeline that moves with you

See approximately nine day columns in portrait. Rotate for more days at the same narrow width, with the top controls hidden to give your roster more room.

Scroll, rotate, explore

Swipe horizontally across saved periods. Keep the same day in view when rotating. Pinch with two fingers to magnify and pan, then release to return to the normal scale. Single taps still open flight details.

Start with today

Optionally place today at the left edge when opening the app, with its date highlighted. Other days remain available to scroll through. The month in view shows its flight-hour total.

The duty, in order

Follow report time, flight number, departure, route, arrival, aircraft type and debrief in a compact matrix. Standby start/end times and turnarounds under three hours appear where the saved data supports them.

From one month to a full year

Automatic retrieval

Enter your roster website address, Crew ID and password at first setup. Sync opens My Schedule and retrieves your selected period, with a progress bar. Request current and next month together when the next month is published.

Saved months, together

Keep downloaded rosters offline. Select multiple saved periods or open current plus next month without syncing again. Future months appear first, followed by current and older months. A new download replaces the same period; older months remain until deleted.

Twelve-month history

Request the current month and previous eleven months in one download. Each available month is saved separately and can be viewed in the combined timeline. Retrieval depends on the roster website’s availability.

Useful detail, one tap away

Flight and duty cards

Tap a flight number for a compact card with local and UTC clocks, flight time, duty information, aircraft type and registration, crew, passengers and gate/bay when available. Swipe between flights in the same duty. Training duties have their own crew and detail cards.

Airport quick references

Tap an airport code to open a compact airport card. Available public information includes runway facts and dated NADP, arrival-route and approach reminders. These are memory aids that direct you to official sources, not current operational procedures.

Period information

Open the information button for condensed hours, statistics, hotel details, memos and expiries. Expiries show code, description and expiry date. Availability depends on the saved roster.

Weather context without another roster sync

Refresh METAR and TAF independently. Origin and destination summaries relate available observations and forecasts to departure and arrival times; tap for the full reports. Highlighted weather indicators draw attention to low visibility, strong winds, icing-related signals and other significant conditions when supported by the source.

Hong Kong ATIS can show the published departure runway in use. Weather, forecasts and ATIS are limited by their issue times and coverage: a current report does not establish conditions for a future flight. Always use an approved briefing and official sources.

Reminders built around report time

One duty

Create a reminder from Flight Details. Even when you choose a later sector, the reminder uses the duty’s report time, not that flight’s departure. Give it a title or let it use the flight/date information.

A simple timing choice

On the report day, choose hours and minutes before report, in 15-minute steps—even if that lands on the previous day. For one or more days before, choose a local time of day and see the calculated reminder date.

All report times

Use the main bell to view or delete programmed notifications and add general rules, such as 1 hour 30 minutes before every report or two days before at 09:00. Notifications require permission and remain subject to iOS delivery settings.

Your data, ready to move

Roster backup and restore

In Settings, export all saved periods and downloaded duty details to one backup file. Import and merge it in another installation without contacting the roster website. Matching periods keep the newer download. Credentials, reminders and preferences are not included.

CrewLounge PILOTLOG

Export a chosen date range or all saved dates as CSV for the PILOTLOG Import Wizard. Use your saved Crew ID to match PIC/SIC roles, and the airline code configured in Settings to prefix flight numbers when needed.

LogTen Pro

Export the same flight data as a CSV for LogTen’s Mac importer. Map flight, time, crew and landing fields before confirming. Additional pilots can use custom crew fields. For iOS-only file import, contact LogTen support.

What is included in the logbook exports?

UTC dates and block clocks, flight numbers, route, duration, aircraft type and registration, PIC/SIC time when identifiable, pilot names with the nominated PIC first, IFR, cross-country, actual instrument, estimated night time, PF/PM and day/night landing counts. Day time is total minus night.

The current export defaults set IFR, cross-country and actual instrument to total time; actual instrument is an assumption, not measured experience. Each duty starts PM and alternates PF/PM, with one landing on PF sectors and zero on PM sectors. Night estimates use civil twilight along a constant-speed great-circle route. Review and correct these defaults, scheduled times and landing counts before importing into a personal logbook. No VFR time is added.

Missing or ambiguous data is flagged. Flight-only exports omit non-flight duties and identified deadhead legs. Check for duplicates in the receiving logbook. Exports include personal crew information: save and share them privately.

Private by design, clear about its limits

Credentials stay in this device’s iOS Keychain. Automatic login requires device authentication. Retrieval runs while the app is open; after saving, eRoster HKG attempts logout and clears its temporary session. Changing the website clears the old credentials and session. Your saved roster stays available.

Explore fictional sample rosters through Settings → Open demo without a company account. Read the usage notices and privacy information in Settings, and delete saved rosters or login credentials whenever you choose.
