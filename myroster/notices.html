Updated 20 November 2026

Official sources always take precedence

Always consult and independently verify the current official airline roster, approved manuals, dispatch information, weather briefings, NOTAMs, ATC instructions and applicable regulations. Do not use this app for flight planning, navigation, dispatch, flight or cabin operations, duty/rest compliance, reporting decisions, or any other safety-critical purpose.

Data can be incomplete or wrong

Imported schedules, crew, times, flight hours, aircraft, gates, passenger counts and airport references may be missing, delayed, outdated, misinterpreted or incorrect. Saved rosters are snapshots, not live notifications. Sync completion does not certify accuracy. Time-zone conversions and calculated totals are supplementary and must be checked against official records.

Weather and runway information

METAR, TAF, ATIS, runway indications and automatically generated alerts are supplementary displays only. A report may be unavailable, stale or outside the time of the flight. An alert is not an exhaustive hazard assessment. No alert does not mean conditions are safe. Current ATIS does not predict the runway that will be assigned; only current official sources and ATC instructions govern.
