myRoster support

Contact aldoapps@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a short description of what happened.

Keep private information private.

Never send your password. Remove crew names, IDs and confidential information from screenshots unless strictly necessary and authorized.

Getting started

  1. Read the usage notices displayed on first launch.
  2. In Settings, save your authorized Crew ID and password.
  3. Choose the period and tap Sync. Authenticate using your device.
  4. Keep the app open while it retrieves and saves the requested months.
  5. Check the result against eCrew. The status reports logout confirmation or local-only session cleanup.

Saved months and history

Use Saved Rosters to select months or open the saved current month and next month together. In the period selector, “Download past 12 months” requests the current month and previous eleven. Availability depends on eCrew. Previously saved periods remain available offline until replaced or deleted.

Connection problems

Check network access and your account in the official eCrew system. If the app requests verification, use Connection, then Resume. If your password changed, save the new credentials in Settings. Do not repeatedly retry a rejected login. Include the displayed error when reporting a problem.

Weather and missing details

Weather refresh is separate from roster sync. PAX, gates, aircraft registration and crew detail availability depend on the source and when you last synced. No display guarantees that information is current or complete. Always consult approved operational sources.

Delete local data

Swipe to delete saved periods or remove all saved rosters in Settings. “Forget credentials and session” separately removes saved login credentials. Neither action changes your airline account.

Beta feedback

TestFlight builds may expire or contain defects. Use TestFlight feedback or email support. Include reproduction steps and the version/build shown in Settings. Never rely on this entertainment-only beta as an official record.
