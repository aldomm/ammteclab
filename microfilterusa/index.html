A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What does a three-stage system filter? +

That depends on the cartridges installed. Polypropylene cartridges target suspended particles, while activated carbon block can address chlorine, taste, and odor. We confirm the selected media, ratings, and suitability for your home before installation.

Is this the same as a water softener? +

No. Filtration and softening serve different purposes. A cartridge filtration system should not be assumed to remove dissolved hardness minerals. Tell us if scale or hard water is your main concern so we can discuss the right approach.

How often will I need to replace the filters? +

Replacement intervals depend on the filter type, incoming water quality, and household usage. We explain the recommended care for your specific system. The filters are replaceable; the system is not maintenance-free.

How much does an installation cost? +

Every home is different. Your quote depends on the system, plumbing access, and installation requirements. Contact us for a tailored estimate and compare both the installation and ongoing cartridge costs.

Do you serve my neighborhood? +

We’re focused on homes in Orlando. Send us your ZIP code to confirm availability and talk through the next steps.
